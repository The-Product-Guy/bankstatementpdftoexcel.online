How to Convert Bank Statements to Excel

Converting bank statement PDFs to Excel doesn't have to be painful. Here's how StatementFlow handles it:

  1. Upload your PDF — drag-and-drop or browse. We accept files up to 500 MB on Enterprise plans.
  2. Automatic detection — our engine detects whether the PDF is text-based (digital) or image-based (scanned) and picks the best extraction strategy.
  3. OCR for scanned PDFs — scanned statements go through PaddleOCR with table-structure detection (img2table) so every cell is captured accurately.
  4. Download your Excel — the result contains two sheets: Raw_Statement (exactly as extracted) and Normalized_Transactions (standardized columns ready for pivot tables).

The entire process typically takes 10-30 seconds for text PDFs and 1-3 minutes for scanned documents.

Tips

5 Excel Tips for Working with Converted Statements

  1. Pivot tables — select your transaction data and insert a PivotTable to instantly summarize spending by category or month.
  2. SUMIFS for totals — use =SUMIFS(Withdrawal_Amount, Description, "*UPI*") to sum all UPI debits.
  3. Conditional formatting — highlight large withdrawals or deposits with color scales to spot anomalies quickly.
  4. Text-to-Columns — if a description field contains multiple details separated by slashes, split them into separate columns for cleaner analysis.
  5. Freeze panes — freeze the header row (View → Freeze Panes) so column names stay visible as you scroll through hundreds of transactions.

Guide

Text-Based vs Scanned PDFs: What's the Difference?

Text-based PDFs are generated digitally by your bank's online portal. The text is embedded in the PDF and can be copied. These convert the fastest and most accurately.

Scanned PDFs are photographs of printed statements. The PDF contains images, not text. These require OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to extract the data, which takes longer but still produces accurate results with our PaddleOCR + img2table pipeline.

How to tell the difference: open the PDF and try to select text with your cursor. If you can highlight individual words, it's text-based. If the entire page selects as one image, it's scanned.
