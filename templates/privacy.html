Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use Statement Converter, we collect:

  • Email address - used for sign-in only. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Uploaded PDF files - processed in memory and deleted immediately after conversion. We do not keep your financial documents.
  • Usage data - conversion counts, page counts, and file sizes to enforce plan limits. No transaction content is stored.
  • Feedback submissions - if you opt in to share a PDF for analysis, it is stored temporarily and deleted after {{ feedback_retention_days }} days.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you via email sign-in link (powered by Resend).
  • To process your PDF and return the converted Excel file.
  • To track usage against your plan quota.
  • To improve extraction accuracy (only with your explicit opt-in).

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate Statement Converter:

  • Resend - for sending sign-in emails.
  • Stripe - for payment processing. We never see or store your card details.
  • OpenAI / Anthropic - for AI-assisted table extraction. Only document structure (headers, column layouts) may be sent; we strip personally identifiable transaction data where possible.
  • Railway - cloud hosting infrastructure.
  • AWS S3 - temporary file storage for conversion results (auto-deleted).

4. Data Retention

  • Uploaded PDFs - deleted immediately after processing.
  • Converted Excel files - stored temporarily (up to 1 hour) for download, then deleted.
  • Account data - retained while your account is active. Contact us to request deletion.
  • Feedback PDFs - deleted after {{ feedback_retention_days }} days if shared.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Files are processed in isolated containers and never written to persistent disk beyond temporary processing. We do not share your data with any third parties beyond what is listed above.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. You may also withdraw feedback PDF sharing consent.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email us at the address listed in your sign-in emails.
